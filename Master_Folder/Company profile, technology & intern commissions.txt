Company profile, technology & intern commissions
Cruze is a deep-tech virtual infrastructure company based in San Antonio, Texas. The company develops software control systems that optimize commercial vehicle speeds in real time to eliminate unnecessary fuel consumption, reduce equipment wear, and bypass highway congestion.

Rather than building physical hardware or changing vehicle engines, Cruze deploys an artificial intelligence layer that integrates directly into existing commercial fleet telematics. The fundamental objective is to transform passive data tracking into active fuel reclamation.

The core technology: PINNs & Swarm AI
To grasp how Cruze creates value, it helps to understand how standard navigation tools fall short. Consumer GPS applications are entirely reactive; they wait for a traffic jam to form and then suggest a detour to route vehicles around it. This approach typically shifts the bottleneck to surrounding roads, failing to solve the systemic problem.

Cruze treats highway traffic as a predictable continuous fluid rather than a random sequence of individual cars.

Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)
Standard machine learning models require massive, continuous historical datasets to identify patterns. Cruze uses Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), which build the physical laws of fluid dynamics and kinematics straight into the AI software. Because the system understands how physical shockwaves move through highway networks, it can accurately forecast where "phantom" traffic jams will form up to 15 minutes before they happen.

50Hz telemetry tracking & active speed sculpting
Once a potential bottleneck is flagged, Cruze coordinates the vehicles through a distributed mesh network:

High-frequency ingestion: The platform processes location and velocity inputs from participating vehicles at a frequency of 50Hz (50 data transmissions per truck, every second).
Swarm coordination: The system acts as a collective swarm intelligence. Instead of changing a vehicle's route, it transmits minute speed recommendations to the cruise control systems of fleet trucks miles upstream from the congestion point.
Dissolving traffic waves: By micro-adjusting vehicle velocities by small percentages, Cruze absorbs and dissolves compression waves before they materialize into a dead stop. Vehicles maintain consistent, efficient forward momentum, eliminating the braking and acceleration cycles that waste diesel.
The Gainshare revenue model
Enterprise software typically requires long sales cycles, large capital expenditures, and budget approvals from cautious executives. Cruze completely sidesteps this friction by operating a risk-free Gainshare Model.

The setup: Cruze installs its system into a fleet's existing onboard hardware for a 30-day verification trial at zero cost to the client.
The billing mechanism: At the close of each cycle, Cruze utilizes historical baselines to verify exactly how much capital the fleet saved on fuel and maintenance expenses. Cruze then takes a percentage of those confirmed savings as revenue.
The buyer incentive: If the software does not successfully lower a fleet's fuel consumption, the client pays zero. Because the software pays for itself entirely out of captured waste, sales friction is effectively eliminated.
How intern commissions work
As an enterprise development intern, your earning potential scales directly with the size of the accounts you bring into the pipeline. When an account you sourced transitions from a pilot into a permanent commercial agreement, you receive a recurring share of the monthly savings generated by that entire fleet.

The payout structure utilizes an accelerated tiering system. This means your share is optimized to give you a strong baseline payout on the initial block of vehicles, while remaining highly scalable as fleet sizes expand:

Tier 1 (Vehicles 1–10): You receive a 15% share of the monthly savings generated by the first 10 trucks in the contract.
Tier 2 (Vehicles 11–50): You receive a 10% share of the monthly savings generated by trucks 11 through 50.
Tier 3 (Vehicles 51+): You receive a 5% share of the monthly savings generated by every single additional truck beyond the 50th vehicle.
On average across major freight routes, the platform reclaims roughly $600 per truck, per month in combined fuel and brake savings. Securing a single mid-sized fleet of 25 trucks translates to a steady monthly recurring commission payout, and landing an enterprise player with 100+ vehicles scales that payout significantly because you collect a percentage across every single truck on the road.
