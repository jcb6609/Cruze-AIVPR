Cruze 101
Cruze builds navigation intelligence for Class 8 truck fleets. The in-cab app gives drivers recommended speeds with optimized routes — fused from traffic-signal data (SPaT), DOT camera feeds, and on-device telemetry — so fleets save fuel, avoid incidents, and keep drivers safe.

The product surfaces
Platform (mobile app) — Flutter in-cab driver app: map navigation, glanceable Speed HUD (current / recommended / posted limit), crash + rollover detection, pothole and braking telemetry, eco "glidepath" approach to signals.
Backend services — main-backend (routing, search, speed advisories) and ml-engine (Python: diagnostics, crash classification).
Camera visualization — globe of live DOT traffic cameras across states (511 feeds).
Website — public marketing site + pilot fleet signup.
Employee Hub — this app: tasks, knowledge, people, the Growth program.
How we talk about the product
Advisory, never directive: we recommend speeds, we never command. "Recommended 52" + context, not "SLOW DOWN". This language rule is audited in the platform repo (docs/strategy/UX-ADVISORY-LANGUAGE-AUDIT.md) and applies to marketing copy too.
